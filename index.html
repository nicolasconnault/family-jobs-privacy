Privacy Policy

Family Jobs — Last updated: 29 April 2026

Family Jobs is a private household chore and pocket-money tracker. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the general public.

Information we collect

  • Camera (CAMERA) — used only to scan QR codes when a child is joining a family. The camera feed is processed entirely on-device; no images or video are stored or transmitted.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS) — used only when a parent chooses to send a child an invite code via SMS. The app opens the system contact picker; the selected phone number is passed directly to the SMS app and is never stored or transmitted to our servers.
  • Device tokens (FCM) — a Firebase Cloud Messaging token is stored on our server so that urgent job notifications can be sent to a child's device. This token is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, location data, or any other personal information beyond what is described above. We do not share any data with third parties for advertising or analytics purposes.

Data storage

App data (family members, jobs, earnings) is stored in a private Supabase database accessible only to authenticated members of that family. Device tokens are stored in the same database and are deleted when a child account is removed.

Children

Family Jobs is designed to be used under parental supervision. Parents create and manage child accounts. No data is knowingly collected from children independently.
